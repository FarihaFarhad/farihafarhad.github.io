❀ About Me

I’m an enthusiastic and driven Computer Science & Engineering student with a strong interest in research, problem-solving, and knowledge sharing. I enjoy exploring challenging computational problems, understanding how intelligent systems work, and turning theoretical ideas into solutions that can be useful in the real world. In the future, I hope to contribute to meaningful research while also sharing what I learn and helping others discover the joy of learning.

My research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Graph Theory & Algorithms, with a particular focus on their real-world applications. I’m especially interested in exploring how these techniques can be used effectively and conveniently to address practical problems and improve everyday experiences. For me, the most exciting part of research is not only developing intelligent methods, but understanding how we can make them useful, accessible, and impactful in real life.

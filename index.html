❀ About Me

I'm an enthusiastic and driven Computer Science & Engineering student with a strong passion for research and knowledge sharing. I love exploring complex problems, contributing to impactful academic work, and one day standing at the front of a classroom helping others fall in love with learning too.

My research wanders across the places where technology meets everyday life — how AI shapes our homes and trust, how social media reshapes families and teenagers, and how smart, sustainable ideas can lift up farmers and small businesses in Bangladesh.
